🧭The recovery plan

In all three incidents the attacker abused a contract-migration exploit to move Garuda staking contracts onto attacker-controlled code and sweep their contents. The contracts’ stake records survived on-chain — that is why every figure on this page can be proven, wallet by wallet.

  • The money trail leads to KuCoin. The stolen DO was sold on Oct 31, and 82,143,104.60 LUNC was deposited to KuCoin’s Terra Classic deposit wallet terra1kmn3sygypc4uszm6ydr22ehw20dpz66kqzs7ph under deposit memos 2097600880 and 2097613661. Those memos are KuCoin deposit IDs tied to KYC accounts — the exchange holds the identification path.
  • Reports are already filed. Freeze and identification reports asking KuCoin to hold those deposits and preserve the account records have been filed by community members and by the DO token owner. Every additional victim report adds weight — file yours below.
  • The wallets to know: Oct 31 drain wallet terra1ktlxt99d08x99wlds9p2j59uh6jy0nww7uq55l (drain transaction 1CF7487E…FC826) · Oct 20/21 attacker wallet terra10ytpt4g5j3c0rmg2qlr6nyryqclcff87elszm6
  • Recovery is not guaranteed. What is guaranteed is the record: your loss is provable on-chain, ready for the exchange, the Garuda team, or law enforcement.

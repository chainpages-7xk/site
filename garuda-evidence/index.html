# Garuda DeFi — Hack Evidence Package (Oct 31, 2026)

Prepared for the Garuda team, KuCoin compliance, and law enforcement. Every item below is verifiable on-chain via the linked transactions. All times ET.

## What happened

Garuda's validator/ops wallet cluster was compromised (team's own statement). Using a standing no-expiry migration grant, the attacker migrated 12 CW20 staking pools to a drain contract that swept their balances, dumped the tokens through the Garuda DEX, and sent the proceeds to KuCoin under two deposit accounts.

On October 31, 2026, twelve Garuda DeFi staking pools were taken over by an attacker and their funds were stolen. Fill in this form and it builds your personal loss report — ready to file with KuCoin, the Garuda team, or law enforcement.

⚠️ Read this first

  • This page never asks you to connect a wallet or sign anything. Anyone who asks for your seed phrase is a scammer.
  • Private by design: everything you type stays on your device. This page sends nothing anywhere — it only writes your report on this screen, for you to copy.
  • You only need your wallet address (the public one, starts with terra1…). Never type a seed phrase or private key anywhere, ever.
